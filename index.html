Enter it once.
It shows up everywhere it should.

The current govisland.com is built almost entirely out of hand-made pages — hundreds of them, each maintained separately. The new site is built around a different idea: structured content. A restaurant, an artwork, or a climate pilot is entered once, tagged with what it is, and the site takes care of showing it on the map, the calendar, and every relevant page.

1

Pages

A small set of hand-crafted pages — the homepage, pillar pages, Plan Your Visit — that tell the Island's story. Roughly 40 of them, down from hundreds.

Content types

Structured entries — Listings, Events, Blog posts, Properties and more — with defined fields instead of freeform pages.

Taxonomies

Tags with rules. What kind of place is it? What's its focus? What amenities does it have? Taxonomies power filters, map layers, and landing pages.
